# Research: 3 Databases Across Different Business Domains

## 1. Chinook Database
- **Business Domain**: B2C Digital Media & Music Store
- **Business Description**: Simulates a digital audio/media retail store (similar to iTunes). It tracks individual customer purchases, track and album catalogs, media formats, and support staff assigned to customers.
- **Core Entities**:
  - Customer: Customer demographics and support representative.
  - Invoice & InvoiceLine: Transaction headers, line items, unit prices, and billing address.
  - Track & Album & Artist: Catalog metadata and media formats.

## 2. Northwind Database
- **Business Domain**: B2B Wholesale Supply Chain & Distribution
- **Business Description**: Simulates a global wholesale grocery and food distributor. It models procurement from international suppliers, internal inventory management, client order fulfillment, and logistics/shipping companies.
- **Core Entities**:
  - Suppliers & Products: Inventory stock levels, reorder thresholds, and unit costs.
  - Orders & Order Details: Multi-item sales records, discount percentages, and shipping destinations.
  - Shippers: Third-party logistics carriers.

## 3. AdventureWorks Database
- **Business Domain**: Enterprise Manufacturing & Global Retail
- **Business Description**: Simulates a multinational manufacturing company producing and selling bicycles, components, and accessories. It tracks raw material procurement, assembly lines, production work orders, and global wholesale/retail sales operations.
- **Core Entities**:
  - Product & WorkOrder: Assembly lines, production costs, and scrap tracking.
  - SalesOrderHeader & SalesOrderDetail: Multi-region sales transactions and margins.
  - Vendor & PurchaseOrder: Raw material vendor management.
