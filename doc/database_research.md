# Database Research for Businesses Analytics

## 1. Chinook (Digital Media Store)
- **Domain**: B2C digital music and media retail.
- **Key Entities**: Customer, Invoice, Track, Album, Artist, Genre, Employee.
- **Business Use Case**: Customer lifetime value, sales performance per rep, and genre popularity by region.

## 2. Northwind (Wholesale & Supply Chain)
- **Domain**: B2B food supply and distribution.
- **Key Entities**: Suppliers, Products, Orders, Order Details, Shippers, Customers.
- **Business Use Case**: Inventory turnover, supplier reliability, and shipping cost analysis.

## 3. AdventureWorks (Manufacturing & Retail)
- **Domain**: Enterprise manufacturing and retail operations.
- **Key Entities**: Products, Work Orders, Vendors, Sales Orders, Territories.
- **Business Use Case**: Factory floor scrap rate, regional profit margins, and supply chain logistics.

## Selected Project Database: Chinook
We selected Chinook because it simulates realistic transactional retail operations while maintaining an accessible schema ideal for Text-to-SQL agent validation.
